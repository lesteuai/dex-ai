Senior Frontend Engineer

About the role

Requirements

  • Five or more years of frontend experience with TypeScript.
  • Deep knowledge of React, HTML and CSS.
  • Experience with automated accessibility testing.
  • Clear written communication in English.

Benefits

  • Annual learning budget of $2,000.
  • Health, dental and vision coverage.
